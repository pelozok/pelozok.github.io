# Surface brief: home (index.html + en/index.html)

Scope: the single-page bilingual CV. Mode: Persuade (recruiters for remote Software/AI Engineer roles decide to contact or download the CV).
Audience: recruiters and engineering managers scanning in under a minute. Action: download CV, email, LinkedIn.
Positioning (user-pinned): a complete software engineer (WordPress, Rails, React, APIs, infrastructure, quality) who now builds with AI. Never sell him as "just workflows and agents". Nothing from his experience is lost: every client project, finance/process background, design tools, E2E testing, certifications, citizenship.
Constraints: static, no build, GitHub Pages, strict meta CSP, ES at / and EN at /en/. User rejected the transit-map/timeline world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The CV as a node canvas: every skill and job is a node, edges show how each one fed the next, and the whole graph converges on Finwaise with "your team" as the next node. Refuses both the dark neon card grid and the "AI workflows guy" pitch.

OWN-WORLD: Light builder canvas (#f4f5f7 with a 22px dot grid inside canvases only), white nodes with 1px rule and soft tinted shadow, 12px radius, kind label in Geist Mono with a small category square (base ink, build blue, ops green, AI orange). Geist display and body, Geist Mono for metadata only. One accent: signal orange #ff4f12, reserved for AI and the Finwaise output. Dark mode is the night canvas.

STORY: First viewport tells "full-stack engineer who ships AI in production"; the graph proves breadth leads somewhere. Then: skills bento, Finwaise case (team project, his involvement), every project grouped, experience and credentials, contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: name H1, role line, 20-word lede, CV button + email link. Right 7/12: career graph canvas, 11 nodes in 4 columns (base, web, platform, AI/output), bezier edges, tokens flowing toward Finwaise. Mobile: the graph becomes a vertical node list with connectors.

FORM: Lienzo de workflows (career graph), candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (pick), seed key dc60b54c. Signature interaction: hovering/focusing a node lights its edges and neighbours and dims the rest; tokens flow along edges (reduced motion: static).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
